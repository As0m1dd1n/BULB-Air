Fixed rules: 0.5° box · each day compared with its month’s normal level · Sen slope · Mann–Kendall test with Hamed–Rao correction · four seasons tested together.

Mann–Kendall asks whether later years are higher than earlier years more often than chance allows. Sen slope = the middle value of the slopes between every pair of years. Hamed–Rao makes it stricter when one year resembles the next.

Data to May 2020: NASA’s open copy on AWS. Later: NASA Giovanni. Files, code, method and change log are in the repository.
