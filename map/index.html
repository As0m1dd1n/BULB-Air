ⓘ How to read this map

Each column is one 0.25° NASA OMI satellite cell (about 25 km). Its height and colour show the NO₂ in the air column above it, averaged over the clear days of the season.

Grey flat cells: fewer than 10 clear days, so no value. White frame: the 4 cells our trend test uses.

Only NO₂ is measured here. Dust and PM2.5 are not part of this dataset, so they are not drawn.

Landmarks: the close-up is a real satellite photo; the yellow ring marks the spot.
